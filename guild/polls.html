How Voting Works

Your vote is your voice

Guild polls are for members with The Syndicate role in Discord. Sign in with your existing Discord account. When voting is enabled, each eligible member can submit once per poll.

Answers are stored separately from participation records. The system does not place Discord IDs inside ballots. As with any online vote, absolute anonymity against all technical logs or timing analysis is not guaranteed.

Votes cannot be changed after submission. Results appear in the archive when published.
