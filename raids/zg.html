Welcome to Zul'Gurub

Zul'Gurub is a 20-player catch-up raid that helps bridge the gap between level-60 dungeons and larger 40-player raids. New 60s are welcome here—come ready to learn, contribute, and have fun.

Rewards & Reputation

Zandalar TribeEarn reputation with the faction based on Yojamba Isle.
EnchantsReputation and raid turn-ins unlock valuable class head, leg, and shoulder enchants.
GearClass armor sets, desirable caster gear, Bloodvine crafting materials, and epic weapons.
Rare MountsSwift Zulian Tiger from Thekal and Swift Razzashi Raptor from Mandokir. Both mounts are open roll.

Good to Know

  • Keep an eye out for coins and bijous. ZG trash can drop Troll Coins and Hakkar Bijous, which can be turned in for Zandalar Tribe reputation.
  • Primal Hakkari Idols are worth planning for. These raid tokens, combined with a class-appropriate Punctured Voodoo Doll from Hoodoo Piles, are used for powerful class enchants. Consider soft reserving an idol if you need one.
  • Yojamba Isle matters. Visit the Zandalar Tribe NPCs for reputation rewards and related turn-ins.

Hard Reserves

Bloodvine is hard reserved for the Syndicate Guild Bank for crafting.

Current ZG guidelines

  • Bloodvine is hard reserved for the Guild Bank.
  • Rare mounts are open roll.
  • Mining is rolled off in-game.
  • Herbing is assigned, with gathered herbs kept for guild potions and Bloodvine crafting.
