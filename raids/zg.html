ZUL'GURUB

ON FARM 20-MAN

Deep within the jungles of Stranglethorn Vale, the Gurubashi trolls have summoned Hakkar the Soulflayer. Zul'Gurub is a 20-player raid featuring Zandalar Tribe reputation, class rewards, profession recipes, enchants, and a collection of encounters ranging from straightforward farm bosses to optional challenges.

RAID SIZE 20 Players
RESET 3 Days
LOCATION Stranglethorn Vale
ATTUNEMENT None
REPUTATION Zandalar Tribe

ABOUT ZUL'GURUB

Zul'Gurub is a 20-player raid centered around Hakkar and the Gurubashi priests who empower him. Defeating the five High Priests weakens Hakkar before the final encounter.

20-MAN 3 DAY RESET NO ATTUNEMENT

ZANDALAR TRIBE

ZG is the primary source of Zandalar Tribe reputation. Reputation unlocks class rewards, profession recipes, consumables, and important head, leg, and shoulder enchants.
