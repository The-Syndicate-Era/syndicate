Welcome to Onyxia's Lair

Located in southern Dustwallow Marsh, Onyxia's Lair is a short, 40-player raid with a single boss: Onyxia. Although the raid itself is brief, every raider must complete their own attunement before entering.

Attunement & Entry

  • The key: Each character must possess a Drakefire Amulet to enter Onyxia's Lair.
  • The bag rule: The amulet must be in your character's inventory bags—not in your bank. Without it in your bags, the portal will block entry.
