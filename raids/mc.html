Welcome to Molten Core

Molten Core is a 40-player raid deep within Blackrock Mountain, home to Ragnaros the Firelord. As one of WoW Classic's original endgame raids, MC offers powerful Tier 1 gear, legendary weapon components, and valuable crafting materials. Whether you're gearing up or chasing that elusive drop, come ready to face the fire!

Rewards & Reputation

Hydraxian WaterlordsEarn reputation by defeating enemies in Molten Core. The faction's quest chain leads to the ability to extinguish the raid's runes.
Legendary WeaponsFind components for Thunderfury, Blessed Blade of the Windseeker and Sulfuras, Hand of Ragnaros.
Tier 1 GearClass-specific armor sets, powerful weapons, and accessories that help prepare you for later raids.
Crafting MaterialsCollect valuable materials for Fire Resistance gear, specialized equipment, and legendary weapons.

Good to Know

  • Get attuned: Complete Attunement to the Core to unlock convenient access without having to travel through Blackrock Depths. See our attunement guide →
  • Fire Resistance matters: Certain encounters benefit from resistance gear, especially for assigned tanks. Follow your raid leader's requirements.
  • Bring fire protection: Greater Fire Protection Potions can help with heavy fire damage, especially during Ragnaros.
  • Hydraxian Waterlords reputation: The faction quest chain provides Aqual Quintessence to extinguish runes before Majordomo Executus. Eternal Quintessence is the reusable upgrade.

Hard Reserves

  • Bindings of the Windseeker: Hard reserved for creation of Thunderfury, Blessed Blade of the Windseeker.
  • Eye of Sulfuras: Hard reserved for creation of Sulfuras, Hand of Ragnaros.
